The room above the room.

The broadcast is low, but it is honest. If you stay still, the dust starts to rhyme.
Listening closer will pin the room long enough to hear the seam between lines.
Use 1, 2, 3, or 4 on the keyboard to switch presets. Press L or Space to pin the signal, then save the living state as a postcard.
Attic band

Warm static, low hum, and a ceiling that remembers footsteps.

The attic channel is all soft edges and close dust. It feels like the house is trying to say something without waking anyone else up.

Hallway loop

Sharper echoes, a little more motion, and the sense of a message coming back changed.

This preset leans into bounce and repetition. The signal feels like it has crossed the landing twice before it reaches the listener.

Moon weather

Cold light, slow drift, and the cleanest ghost in the band.

The moon line is still haunted, but it is a courteous sort of haunting. It comes in clear enough to leave a postcard behind.

Why it exists

Because not every public page should behave like a brochure.

Ghost Radio is intentionally small, strange, and inspectable. It is built as a standalone public repo so the weird lane stays visible, shareable, and easy to reuse without dragging a framework or a build pipeline into the room.
