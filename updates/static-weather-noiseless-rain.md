# Static Weather, Noiseless Rain

> The forecast arrived by memorandum, unsigned and damp.

The clouds filed a brief at the mailbox in static.
At noon, the windows learned how to shiver quietly.

- Humidity: 89
- Chance of precipitation: 100% (invisible)
- Chance of thunder: 0% (muted)

Every raindrop passed as a rumor.
By nightfall, the street was wet with facts that were never spoken.
