# Weather-Thick Mnemonic

> The storm waited for the meeting to adjourn.

The rain came with an agenda and a backup battery.
At noon, the wind changed its name three times and blamed the thermostat.

- Pressure: 1019 (nervous)
- Chance of precipitation: 87% (possibly)
- Chance of reason: 2% (unavailable)

By dusk, every puddle had become an archive clerk,
indexing yesterday’s thunder into stamped folders and passing it back at the curb.
