# Static Weather, Quiet Latitude

> The sky keeps two clocks: one for rain and one for thought.

A forecast came in, but it wore a shoelace and a borrowed coat.
The wind changed its argument three times before noon and then called it a
method.

- Pressure: 1022 (self-interrogating)
- Haze: 72% (philosophically ambiguous)
- Chance of mercy: 11% (requires patience)

By evening, the streetlights were translating static into a tiny theology:
not of belief, but of careful doubt.
